# v3 — Çevre, sağlık ve yaşam tarzı

> **Alan:** Kelime (5 konudan 3.) — sınav yazma görevlerinin en olası konusu
> **Süre:** ~30 dakika okuma + 20 dakika pratik
> **Test:** [`v3-test.html`](v3-test.html) · 15 soru

---

## Neden bu konu

"İklim değişikliği için birey ne yapabilir?", "Fast food vergilendirilmeli mi?", "Şehirlerde bisiklet
yolları" — B2 essay konularının belki yarısı bu iki temadadır. Ayrıca sağlıkla ilgili kelimeler günlük
hayatta da en çok ihtiyaç duyulanlardır (doktora gitmek, semptom anlatmak).

**Büyük fikir:** Bu temada kelimeler **problem → sebep → çözüm** zinciri halinde öğrenilir; essay yapısı
zaten bu zincirdir.

| İfade | Anlam | Örnek |
|---|---|---|

| **sustainable / sustainability** | sürdürülebilir | *a **sustainable** transport policy* |
| **recycle / reuse / reduce** | geri dönüştür / yeniden kullan / azalt | — |
| **energy-efficient** | enerji verimli | *an **energy-efficient** boiler* |
| **eco-friendly / environmentally friendly** | çevre dostu | — |

| **offset (emissions)** | dengelemek | — |
| **incentive / subsidy / tax on** | teşvik / sübvansiyon / vergi | *offer **incentives** for electric cars* |
| **raise awareness of** | farkındalık yaratmak | — |
| **impose a ban on / phase out** | yasaklamak / kademeli kaldırmak | *They **phased out** coal by 2030.* |

## 3. ⭐ Sağlık ve hastalık

| İfade | Anlam | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **symptom / diagnose / diagnosis** | belirti / teşhis koymak | *She **was diagnosed with** diabetes.* |
| **treatment / cure / prescription** | tedavi / çare / reçete | *a course of **treatment*** |
| **prescribe / take medication** | reçete etmek / ilaç almak | — |
| **side effect** | yan etki | — |
| **contagious / infectious** | bulaşıcı | — |
| **chronic / acute** | kronik / akut | *a **chronic** condition* |
| **recover from / make a recovery** | iyileşmek | *He **recovered from** the operation.* |
| **injury / sprain / bruise / fracture** | yaralanma / burkulma / çürük / kırık | — |
| **GP (general practitioner) / surgeon / specialist** | aile hekimi / cerrah | — |
| **appointment / check-up** | randevu / kontrol | *book an **appointment*** |
| **be off sick / sick leave** | raporlu olmak | — |
| **painkiller / antibiotics** | ağrı kesici / antibiyotik | — |

> ⚠️ **Tuzak:** *I'm ill* / *I feel ill* ✓ · *I have a headache / a cold / the flu* ✓ ·
> *I am headache* ✗. Ayrıca `medicine` (ilaç, sayılamaz genel) ≠ `medication` ≠ `drug` (ilaç ya da uyuşturucu).

## 4. Yaşam tarzı, beslenme ve spor

| İfade | Anlam | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **a balanced diet** | dengeli beslenme | *stick to a **balanced diet*** |
| **go on a diet / cut down on** | diyete girmek / azaltmak | *I've **cut down on** sugar.* |
| **give up / quit** | bırakmak | *He **gave up** smoking.* |
| **work out / exercise regularly** | spor yapmak | — |
| **keep fit / get into shape** | formda kalmak | — |
| **sedentary lifestyle** | hareketsiz yaşam | — |
| **obesity / overweight** | obezite / fazla kilolu | *rising **obesity** rates* |
| **processed food / junk food** | işlenmiş gıda | — |
| **nutritious / nutrient** | besleyici / besin | — |
| **cope with stress / burn out** | stresle başa çıkmak / tükenmek | *She **burned out** after two years.* |
| **get enough sleep / insomnia** | yeterli uyku / uykusuzluk | — |
| **wellbeing / mental health** | iyi olma hâli / ruh sağlığı | — |

**Faydalı collocation'lar:** *lead a healthy lifestyle · take up a sport · have a check-up ·
put on / lose weight · raise awareness · tackle a problem.*

> ⚠️ **Tuzak:** `cut down on` (azaltmak) ≠ `cut out` (tamamen bırakmak) ≠ `give up` (bırakmak).
> Ayrıca `take up a sport` (başlamak) ≠ `take on work` (üstlenmek).

## 5. Problem–çözüm cümle kalıpları

| İşlev | Kalıp |
|---|---|
| Problemi kurma | *X **poses a serious threat to** Y.* · *X **is on the rise**.* |
| Sebep | *This is largely **due to** …* · *The main cause **lies in** …* |
| Sonuç | *This **has led to** a sharp rise in …* |
| Çözüm önerme | *One way to **tackle** this would be to …* · *Governments **should introduce** …* |
| Karşı görüş | *Critics **argue that** such measures are unrealistic.* |
| Sonuç bağlama | *Unless action **is taken**, the situation **will only get worse**.* |

## Sık karıştırılanlar — tek tabloda

| Soruda geçen | Doğru cevap | Neden diğeri değil |
|---|---|---|
| *We must protect ______ environment.* | **the** | Sabit kullanım |
| *She was ______ with asthma.* | **diagnosed** | `diagnose sb with` |
| *He is recovering ______ surgery.* | **from** | Edat sabit |
| *I've ______ on caffeine, not stopped completely.* | **cut down** | `cut out` = tamamen |
| *He ______ smoking last year.* | **gave up / quit** | Tamamen bırakmak |
| *a ______ lifestyle: no exercise at all* | **sedentary** | — |
| *The drug has serious ______ .* | **side effects** | — |
| *rising ______ rates among children* | **obesity** | İsim gerekli |
| *We should ______ awareness of recycling.* | **raise** | `rise` nesne almaz |
| *Energy prices have ______ sharply.* | **risen** | `raise` özneli nesne ister |
| *a ______ energy source such as wind* | **renewable** | — |

`rise / raise` ayrımı: **rise** (rose, risen) nesnesizdir — *prices rise*; **raise** (raised) nesne alır —
*raise prices / raise awareness*.

## 🖥 Pratik — Problem–çözüm kur

> **Amaç:** Essay yapısının kelime altyapısını kurmak · **Süre:** 20 dk
> **💸 Maliyet:** Yok

### Adımlar
1. Doğru kelimeyi seç:
   1. Cities should invest in ______ (renewable / recyclable) energy.
   2. He was ______ (diagnosed / prescribed) with a chronic condition.
   3. I've ______ (cut down on / cut out) salt, but I still use a little.
   4. Air pollution ______ (poses / makes) a serious threat to public health.
   5. Awareness of plastic waste has ______ (risen / raised) considerably.
   6. A ______ (sedentary / sustainable) lifestyle increases the risk of heart disease.
2. Her problem için **bir sebep ve bir çözüm** cümlesi yaz (İngilizce):
   - *Traffic congestion in big cities*
   - *Rising obesity among young people*
   - *Plastic waste in the sea*
3. **Yazma görevi (120–150 kelime):** *Some people think individuals can do little about climate change;
   it is governments that must act. Discuss both views and give your opinion.*
   Zorunlu: `carbon footprint`, `incentive`, `impose a ban on`, `unless action is taken`, `on balance`.

<details>
<summary><b>Cevap anahtarı</b></summary>

1. **renewable** 2. **diagnosed** 3. **cut down on** 4. **poses** 5. **risen** 6. **sedentary**

Örnek sebep-çözüm: *Congestion is largely due to the lack of reliable public transport.
One way to tackle this would be to introduce congestion charges and invest the revenue in metro lines.*

**Örnek metin:** *It is often argued that individual action is symbolic: however carefully I reduce my
**carbon footprint**, one household cannot offset industrial emissions. Governments, by contrast, can
**impose a ban on** single-use plastics or offer **incentives** for renewable energy, and these measures
affect millions at once. On the other hand, policies rarely appear without public pressure, and consumer
choices shape what companies produce. **Unless action is taken** at both levels, targets will keep being
missed. **On balance**, I believe governments carry the greater responsibility, but individual behaviour
is what makes ambitious policies politically possible.*

**Değerlendirme ölçütü:** (1) İki görüş de tartışıldı mı? (2) Beş zorunlu ifade doğru kalıpta mı?
(3) Sonuç paragrafı kendi görüşünü net veriyor mu? (4) `rise/raise` doğru kullanıldı mı?
</details>

## Nasıl karşına çıkar — örnek kalıplar

**Kalıp 1 — Collocation**
> *Governments should offer ______ for electric vehicles.* → **incentives**

**Kalıp 2 — rise/raise**
> *Find the error: "Sea levels have raised by 20 cm."* → **risen**

**Kalıp 3 — Edat**
> *She was diagnosed ______ a rare disorder.* → **with**

**Kalıp 4 — Anlam derecesi**
> *Which means you stopped completely?* (a) *I cut down on meat.* (b) *I cut out meat.* → **(b)**

**Kalıp 5 — Word formation**
> *The policy is not ______ in the long term. (SUSTAIN)* → **sustainable**

---

## 60 saniyelik özet

- Problem kelimeleri: emissions, carbon footprint, deforestation, drought, landfill, extinction.
- Çözüm kelimeleri: renewable, sustainable, energy-efficient, incentive, phase out, raise awareness.
- `protect **the** environment`; `pollution` sayılamaz; `emissions` çoğul.
- Sağlık: diagnose sb **with**, recover **from**, side effect, chronic/acute, be off sick.
- Yaşam tarzı: balanced diet, cut down on ≠ cut out, take up a sport, sedentary, burn out.
- `rise` nesnesiz (prices rise) / `raise` nesneli (raise awareness).
- Problem–çözüm kalıpları essay iskeletini kurar: *poses a threat to · is due to · one way to tackle this*.
- `Unless action is taken, …` güçlü bir kapanış cümlesidir.
