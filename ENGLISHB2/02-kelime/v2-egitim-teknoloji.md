# v2 — Eğitim, teknoloji ve medya

> **Alan:** Kelime (5 konudan 2.) — sınav metinlerinin en sık iki teması
> **Süre:** ~30 dakika okuma + 20 dakika pratik
> **Test:** [`v2-test.html`](v2-test.html) · 15 soru

---

## Neden bu konu

B2 okuma ve yazma görevlerinin büyük kısmı bu iki temadan gelir: online eğitim, sınav sistemleri,
sosyal medyanın etkisi, yapay zekâ, dijital bağımlılık. Bu temada kelime bilmek yetmez; **tartışma dili**
gerekir — bir eğilimi tarif etmek, bir etkiyi adlandırmak, iki tarafı karşılaştırmak.

**Büyük fikir:** Tema kelimeleri üçlü öğrenilir: **isim + fiil + sıfat** (*education / educate / educational*).
Sınav sık sık aynı kökün başka türünü ister (bkz. `v5` word formation).

## 1. ⭐ Eğitim

| İfade | Anlam | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **take / sit an exam** | sınava girmek | *I'm **sitting** the exam in June.* |
| **pass / fail / resit an exam** | geçmek / kalmak / tekrar girmek | *He had to **resit** the paper.* |
| **revise / revision** | tekrar etmek (İng.) | *I've been **revising** all week.* |
| **enrol on / in a course** | kayıt olmak | *She **enrolled on** a design course.* |
| **degree / undergraduate / postgraduate** | derece / lisans / lisansüstü | *a **degree in** economics* |
| **major in** (Am.) / **specialise in** | uzmanlaşmak | — |
| **tuition fees / scholarship / grant** | öğrenim ücreti / burs | *He won a full **scholarship**.* |
| **curriculum / syllabus** | müfredat / ders içeriği | — |
| **assignment / coursework / dissertation** | ödev / dönem çalışması / tez | *submit an **assignment*** |
| **drop out (of)** | okulu bırakmak | *He **dropped out** in his second year.* |
| **graduate (from)** | mezun olmak | *She **graduated from** Boğaziçi.* |
| **lifelong learning** | yaşam boyu öğrenme | — |
| **distance / online learning** | uzaktan eğitim | — |

> ⚠️ **Tuzak:** `graduate **from** a university` — Türkçedeki "üniversiteyi bitirmek" nesne alır ama
> İngilizcede edat gerekir. Ayrıca `a degree **in** economics` (`of` değil).

## 2. ⭐ Teknoloji

| İfade | Anlam | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **device / gadget** | cihaz | *a wearable **device*** |
| **user-friendly** | kullanımı kolay | *a **user-friendly** interface* |
| **cutting-edge / state-of-the-art** | son teknoloji | — |
| **outdated / obsolete** | eskimiş / kullanılmaz hâle gelmiş | *The software is **obsolete**.* |
| **upgrade / update** | yükseltmek / güncellemek | *I **upgraded** my phone.* |
| **install / uninstall** | kurmak / kaldırmak | — |
| **crash / freeze / glitch** | çökmek / donmak / arıza | *The system **crashed** twice.* |
| **back up (v) / backup (n)** | yedeklemek | *Always **back up** your files.* |
| **data breach / hacking** | veri ihlali | — |
| **artificial intelligence (AI) / algorithm** | yapay zekâ / algoritma | — |

| **coverage** | haber kapsamı | *extensive **coverage** of the election* |
| **headline / article / column** | başlık / makale / köşe yazısı | — |
| **broadcast (v/n)** | yayın yapmak | — |
| **biased / objective** | taraflı / tarafsız | *a **biased** report* |
| **fake news / misinformation** | yalan haber / yanlış bilgi | — |
| **censorship / freedom of the press** | sansür / basın özgürlüğü | — |
| **go viral** | viral olmak | *The clip **went viral** overnight.* |
| **post / share / follow / block** | paylaşmak / takip etmek | — |
| **influencer / content creator** | — | — |
| **screen time** | ekran süresi | *reduce your **screen time*** |
| **privacy settings** | gizlilik ayarları | — |

## 4. Tartışma dili — etki ve eğilim anlatma

| İşlev | İfadeler | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| Artış/azalış | rise, increase, soar, decline, drop, fall | *Screen time has **soared** since 2020.* |
| Etki | have an impact/effect **on**, affect, lead to, result in, contribute to | *Social media **has a profound impact on** teenagers.* |
| Sebep | be caused by, stem from, be due to | *The problem **stems from** poor design.* |
| Avantaj/dezavantaj | benefit, drawback, downside, advantage | *The main **drawback** is the cost.* |
| Genelleme | tend to, on the whole, by and large | *Students **tend to** use their phones in class.* |
| Yumuşatma (hedging) | may, seem to, arguably, to some extent | *This is **arguably** the biggest change.* |

> ⚠️ **Tuzak:** `affect` fiil, `effect` isim. *This will **affect** sales.* / *the **effect** on sales*.
> (`effect` fiil olarak da vardır ama "hayata geçirmek" anlamındadır: *effect change*.)

## 5. Kelime aileleri (word families)

| İsim | Fiil | Sıfat |
|---|---|---|
| education | educate | educational / educated |
| technology | — | technological / technical |
| innovation | innovate | innovative |
| dependence | depend | dependent / dependable |
| competition | compete | competitive |
| addiction | be addicted | addictive / addicted |
| access | access | accessible |
| commerce | — | commercial |
| information | inform | informative / informed |

> `addictive` (bağımlılık yapan — nesne) ≠ `addicted` (bağımlı olmuş — kişi):
> *The game is **addictive**.* / *He is **addicted to** the game.*

## 🖥 Pratik — Tema kelimesini üretime çevir

> **Amaç:** Tartışma dilini kurmak · **Süre:** 20 dk
> **💸 Maliyet:** Yok

### Adımlar
1. Doğru kelimeyi seç:
   1. He ______ (dropped out / graduated) after two years without a degree.
   2. Streaming apps are extremely ______ (addicted / addictive).
   3. The new policy will ______ (affect / effect) all part-time students.
   4. Our office computers are ______ (outdated / obsolete) but still usable.
   5. She won a ______ (scholarship / tuition) that covers all her fees.
   6. Poor design ______ (stems / results) from rushing the launch.
2. Kelime ailesi: köşeli parantezdeki kelimeyi doğru türe çevir.
   1. There has been remarkable [TECHNOLOGY] ______ progress in ten years.
   2. Older users often find the app less [ACCESS] ______ .
   3. Many teenagers show signs of [ADDICT] ______ to short videos.
   4. The lecture was surprisingly [INFORM] ______ .
3. **Yazma görevi (100–120 kelime):** *Should schools ban smartphones in class?*
   Zorunlu: `have an impact on`, `tend to`, `the main drawback`, `arguably`, `on the whole`.

<details>
<summary><b>Cevap anahtarı</b></summary>

1. **dropped out** 2. **addictive** 3. **affect** 4. **outdated** 5. **scholarship** 6. **stems**
2. **technological** · **accessible** · **addiction** · **informative**

**Örnek metin:** *Smartphones **have** a significant **impact on** concentration, and students **tend to**
check them even during tests. **The main drawback** of a total ban, however, is that phones are also
useful learning tools: dictionaries, timers and translation apps are all in one device. **Arguably**,
the problem is not the technology but the lack of clear rules. **On the whole**, I would allow phones
in specific lessons and keep them locked away in others, rather than banning them outright.*

**Değerlendirme ölçütü:** (1) Beş zorunlu ifade de doğru kalıpta mı (*impact **on***)? (2) `affect/effect`
doğru tür mü? (3) Görüş tek yönlü mü, yoksa iki taraf da tartışılmış mı — B2 yazma iki tarafı ister.
</details>

## Nasıl karşına çıkar — örnek kalıplar

**Kalıp 1 — Edat**
> *She has a degree ______ mechanical engineering.* → **in**

**Kalıp 2 — Word formation**
> *The course was highly ______ (BENEFIT).* → **beneficial**

**Kalıp 3 — affect / effect**
> *Find the error: "The new rule will effect everyone."* → **affect**

**Kalıp 4 — Collocation**
> *The video ______ viral within hours.* → **went**

**Kalıp 5 — Anlam ayrımı**
> *Which describes a person, not a thing: addicted or addictive?* → **addicted**

## 60 saniyelik özet

- Eğitim zinciri: enrol on → sit/take an exam → pass/fail/resit → graduate from → drop out.
- `a degree in X`, `graduate from Y` — edatlar sabittir.
- Teknoloji: user-friendly, cutting-edge, outdated/obsolete, crash, back up, data breach.
- `rely/depend **on**`; `heavily dependent`, `widely used` gibi zarf eşleşmelerini birlikte öğren.
- Medya: coverage, biased, censorship, go viral, screen time, misinformation.
- `media` çoğul, `information` sayılamaz.
- `affect` fiil / `effect` isim · `addictive` nesne / `addicted` kişi.
- Tartışma dili: have an impact on, lead to, stem from, drawback, tend to, arguably.
