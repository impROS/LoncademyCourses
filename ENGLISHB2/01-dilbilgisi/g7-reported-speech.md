# g7 — Dolaylı anlatım (reported speech)

> **Alan:** Dilbilgisi (10 konudan 7.) — başkasının sözünü aktarma; g1 ve g2'ye dayanır
> **Süre:** ~25 dakika okuma + 20 dakika pratik
> **Test:** [`g7-test.html`](g7-test.html) · 15 soru

---

## Neden bu konu

Bir toplantıyı özetlemek, bir haberi aktarmak, bir şikâyeti iletmek — hepsi dolaylı anlatımdır.
Sınavda iki yerde çıkar: **cümle dönüştürme** (doğrudan → dolaylı) ve **reporting verb seçimi**
(*He said he would… / He promised to… / He apologised for…*). İkinci kısım B2'yi B1'den ayırır:
`say` ve `tell` dışında fiil kullanabiliyor musun?

**Büyük fikir:** Dolaylı anlatımda üç şey kayar — **zaman**, **kişi** ve **yer/zaman zarfları**.

---

## 1. ⭐ Zaman kaydırma (backshift)

Aktarma fiili geçmişse (`said`, `told`), aktarılan cümlenin zamanı bir adım geriye gider.

| Doğrudan | Dolaylı |
|---|---|
| present simple → | past simple |
| present continuous → | past continuous |
| present perfect → | past perfect |
| past simple → | past perfect |
| will → | would |
| can → | could |
| may → | might |
| must → | had to |
| am/is/are going to → | was/were going to |

> *"I **am** tired." → He said he **was** tired.*
> *"I **have finished**." → She said she **had finished**.*
> *"I **will** call you." → He said he **would** call me.*

**Kaymayanlar:** `would, could, might, should, ought to, used to, past perfect` zaten geridedir.

> ⚠️ **Tuzak:** Aktarma fiili **present** ise kaydırma yapılmaz: *He **says** he **is** tired.*
> Ayrıca hâlâ geçerli bir genel gerçek kaydırılmayabilir: *She said water **boils** at 100°C.*

---

## 2. Kişi, yer ve zaman kaymaları

| Doğrudan | Dolaylı |
|---|---|
| I / we | he / she / they |
| my / our | his / her / their |
| this / these | that / those |
| here | there |
| now | then / at that time |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| last week | the week before |
| next month | the following month |
| ago | before |

> *"I'll see you **here tomorrow**." → He said he'd see me **there the next day**.*

---

## 3. ⭐ say vs tell (ve diğerleri)

| Fiil | Yapı | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **say** | say (**to sb**) that… | *He **said (to me) that** he was busy.* |
| **tell** | tell **sb** that… | *He **told me that** he was busy.* |
| **ask** | ask sb (**to do** / **if** / wh-) | *She **asked me to wait**.* |
| **speak / talk** | speak **to** sb **about** sth | *I spoke to her about it.* |

> ⚠️ **Tuzak:** *He said me* ✗ · *He told that* ✗ (nesnesiz `tell` olmaz — kalıplar hariç: *tell the truth,
> tell a lie, tell a story, tell the time*).

---

## 4. Soru, emir ve rica aktarımı

| Tür | Kalıp | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **Evet/hayır sorusu** | asked + **if / whether** + düz cümle | *"Are you coming?" → He asked **if I was coming**.* |
| **Wh- sorusu** | asked + wh- + düz cümle | *"Where do you live?" → She asked **where I lived**.* |
| **Emir** | told sb **to** + V1 | *"Sit down." → He **told me to sit down**.* |
| **Olumsuz emir** | told sb **not to** + V1 | *"Don't touch it." → He **told me not to touch** it.* |
| **Rica** | asked sb **to** + V1 | *"Could you help?" → She **asked me to help**.* |

> ⚠️ **Tuzak:** Dolaylı soruda **soru sırası bozulur ve `do/does/did` düşer**:
> *He asked me where **did I live*** ✗ → *…where **I lived**.* ✓ Soru işareti de yoktur.

---

## 5. Reporting verbs — B2'nin asıl konusu ⭐

Aynı cümleyi `said` ile aktarmak B1'dir; fiili seçmek B2'dir.

| Yapı | Fiiller | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **verb + to V1** | offer, promise, refuse, agree, threaten, claim | *He **promised to call**.* |
| **verb + sb + to V1** | advise, ask, tell, warn, remind, encourage, persuade, invite, order | *She **advised me to see** a doctor.* |
| **verb + V-ing** | suggest, recommend, deny, admit, mention, propose | *He **denied taking** the file.* |
| **verb + prep + V-ing** | apologise **for**, insist **on**, accuse sb **of**, congratulate sb **on**, blame sb **for**, warn sb **against** | *She **apologised for being** late.* |
| **verb + that** | explain, claim, complain, admit, deny, point out, insist | *He **complained that** the room was cold.* |

Sık kullanılanların anlamı:

| Doğrudan söz | Aktarım |
|---|---|
| *"Please don't tell anyone."* | He **begged** me **not to tell** anyone. |
| *"You should rest."* | She **advised** me **to rest**. |
| *"It wasn't me."* | He **denied** doing it. / He denied **that** he had done it. |
| *"Yes, I broke it."* | He **admitted** breaking it. |
| *"Let's take a break."* | She **suggested taking** a break. / She suggested **(that) we take** a break. |
| *"I'll definitely pay you back."* | He **promised to pay** me back. |
| *"Don't go near the edge!"* | She **warned** me **not to go** near the edge. |

> ⚠️ **Tuzak:** *suggest me to do* ✗ → *suggest **doing*** ya da *suggest **that I do*** ✓

## Sık karıştırılanlar — tek tabloda

| Soruda geçen | Doğru cevap | Neden diğeri değil |
|---|---|---|
| *He ______ me that he was tired.* | **told** | `say` nesne almaz |
| *He ______ that he was tired.* | **said** | `tell` nesnesiz olmaz |
| *"I saw her." → He said he ______ her.* | **had seen** | past simple → past perfect |
| *"I'll help." → He said he ______ help.* | **would** | will → would |
| *"I must go." → He said he ______ go.* | **had to** | must → had to |
| *She asked where ______ .* | **I worked** | Dolaylı soruda düz sıra, `did` düşer |
| *He asked ______ I was ready.* | **if / whether** | Evet-hayır sorusu |
| *She ______ me not to wait.* | **told / asked** | Emir/rica kalıbı `to V1` |
| *He ______ taking the money.* | **denied / admitted** | Bu fiiller V-ing alır |
| *She apologised ______ being late.* | **for** | Edatlı kalıp |
| *He suggested ______ a taxi.* | **taking** | `suggest` + V-ing; `to take` ✗ |

## Nasıl karşına çıkar — örnek kalıplar

**Kalıp 1 — Zaman kaydırma**
> *"I have already paid." → He said he ______ already ______ .* → **had … paid**

**Kalıp 2 — Dolaylı soru sırası**
> *Find the error: "She asked me when would I finish."* → **when I would finish**

**Kalıp 3 — Fiil kalıbı seçimi**
> *He ______ stealing the documents.* (a) refused (b) denied (c) promised → **(b) denied**

**Kalıp 4 — Edatlı fiil**
> *They accused him ______ leaking the report.* → **of**

**Kalıp 5 — Dönüştürme (key word)**
> *"You'd better see a dentist," she said to me.* (**ADVISED**) → *She **advised me to see** a dentist.*

## 60 saniyelik özet

- Aktarma fiili geçmişse zaman bir adım geri kayar; `would/could/might/should/used to` kaymaz.
- `must` → `had to`; present perfect ve past simple → past perfect.
- Kişi, yer ve zaman zarfları da kayar: *here → there*, *tomorrow → the next day*.
- `say` nesne almaz, `tell` nesne ister.
- Dolaylı soruda düz cümle sırası kullanılır, `do/does/did` düşer, soru işareti yoktur.
- Emir → `told sb to V1`, olumsuz emir → `told sb not to V1`.
- Reporting verb kalıpları: `to V1` · `sb to V1` · `V-ing` · `prep + V-ing` · `that`.
- `suggest/recommend/deny/admit` → V-ing; `suggest sb to do` her zaman hatalıdır.

## Kendini kontrol (kâğıda yaz)

1. Aktarma fiili present ise ne değişir?
2. `must` dolaylı anlatımda neye dönüşür? `should` neden dönüşmez?
3. *"Where do you work?"* sorusunu dolaylı hale getirirken iki şey değişir — nedir?
4. `deny`, `apologise`, `advise` fiillerinin aldığı kalıpları yaz.
5. *"Let's leave early."* cümlesini `suggest` ile iki farklı doğru biçimde aktar.
